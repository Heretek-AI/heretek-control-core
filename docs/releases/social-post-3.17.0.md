EMCP Tools 3.17.0 is out 🎉

Your WordPress sites use different builders. Now your AI workflow can work with more of them through the same MCP connection.

This release adds dedicated integrations for Bricks, Breakdance, Avada, Divi 5, Thrive Architect, Oxygen 6, Kirki, WPBakery, Beaver Builder and Visual Composer, alongside Elementor and BeBuilder.

Pick one standalone builder in the new Page Builders screen. Gutenberg stays available beside it, with independent toggles for Spectra, Kadence Blocks, GenerateBlocks, Blocksy Blocks and Otter Blocks.

That is 12 standalone integrations, Gutenberg and 5 block packs. Theme settings stay separate, and each integration has its own tools and bundled authoring guidance. Supported versions and available features vary by builder.

Also in 3.17.0:
✅ Data-powered custom Elementor widgets with queries, supported shortcodes and remote sources
✅ Better History recording and verified, operation-specific undo
✅ Builder skills in discovery and downloadable bundles
✅ Fixed Templates menu visibility

Spectra and Kadence Blocks integrations are included in EMCP Free. The new standalone integrations and other three block packs require EMCP Pro. Vendor licenses remain separate.

Update your plugin, reconnect your MCP client, and select the builder you want to use.
